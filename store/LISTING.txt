Description (paste as-is):
---8<---
Zoom Inherit makes a tab you open from a page start at that page's zoom level.

Chrome remembers zoom per website. If you've zoomed a page to 150% and open a link to a different site, the new tab opens at 100% and you have to zoom again. With Zoom Inherit, the new tab opens at 150%.

HOW IT WORKS
• Works with links that open new tabs, middle-click, Ctrl/Cmd+click, "Open link in new tab", and pages that open new windows.
• The new tab gets its own zoom level, so the zoom Chrome saves for each website is never changed.
• The zoom stays with the new tab as it navigates. Zoom in or out there and that becomes the tab's level, which tabs opened from it inherit in turn.
• Tabs you open yourself (Ctrl+T, bookmarks, typing in the address bar) behave exactly as before.

PRIVACY
No settings, no account, no network access. Zoom Inherit keeps a short list of open tab IDs and their zoom levels in session storage, which Chrome clears when you close the browser. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

NOTE
Turn off other zoom extensions. Two extensions controlling zoom at the same time will conflict.

Permission justifications:
  tabs:           Reads and sets zoom levels on tabs, and identifies which tab opened a new tab (including skipping blank new-tab pages).

  storage:        Keeps the inherited zoom for each open tab in session storage. Nothing is stored after the browser closes or sent anywhere.
